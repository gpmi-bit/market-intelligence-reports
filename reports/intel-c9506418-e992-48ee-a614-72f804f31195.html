SEPTEMBER 2026 COMPETITOR BRIEFING: BATTERY & AUDIO STREAMS

HEADLINE

VARTA AG advances factory closures amid insolvency proceedings, while Sonos and Beats launch flagship premium audio hardware to recover market share.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The battery sector in September was characterized by structural shifts, highlighted by Varta AG’s factory closure and ZincFive’s move toward a public listing via a business combination. Chinese manufacturers are pivoting toward higher-density technologies, with Sichuan Changhong investing in full-tab production lines and the national government prioritizing all-solid-state battery commercialization by 2030. In the audio stream, M&A activity remains prominent following Bose’s acquisition of Firelight Technologies and the ongoing integration of VerVent into Barco NV.

BATTERY

Changhong

  • Capacity Expansion: Subsidiary Changhong Sanjie announced an investment of 287 million RMB to construct a new 21700 full-tab lithium battery production line to alleviate capacity bottlenecks and target high-rate output markets (2026-09-09 Announcement, News).
  • Financial Position: The company’s asset-liability ratio has reached 74.56%, leading to concerns regarding short-term debt pressure amid a general decline in lithium industry margins (2026-09-18 News).
  • Product Strategy: The firm will showcase three major battery product lines at the 140th Canton Fair starting October 15, 2026, targeting international trade in the new energy sector (2026-09-29 News).

Eveready India

  • Market Rating: Analysts downgraded the stock from "Hold" to "Sell" on September 15, 2026, citing long-term underperformance and deteriorating technical indicators despite recent positive financial results (2026-09-16 News).
  • Equity Performance: The share price declined 8.71% during the third week of September, falling from ₹362.75 to ₹331.15 (2026-09-19 News).

Highpower

  • Investment Fund: Approved the establishment of an industrial investment fund in partnership with professional institutions to support sector growth (2026-09-10 Announcement).
  • Guarantees: Total external guarantees for subsidiaries reached 5.49 billion RMB, representing 156.41% of the company’s most recent audited net assets as of late September (2026-09-29 News).

Nippo

  • Dividends: Indo National Limited (Nippo) traded ex-dividend in mid-September (2026-09-17 News).

Panasonic

  • R&D Hub: Opened the "Energy Innovation Square" in Osaka, Japan, a dedicated facility for lithium-ion battery research focused on automotive and data center applications (2026-09-16 News).
  • Investment Pivot: The company is redirecting capital toward batteries and AI infrastructure; management confirmed an increased focus on Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC) technology for grid storage and EVs through 2035 (2026-09-30 News).

Suppo

  • Operations Update: Following a 3-day limit up in stock price, the company confirmed that production of lithium-ion and nickel-hydrogen batteries remains normal and is proceeding based on current orders (2026-09-30 News).
  • Valuation: As of September 28, the company reported a Price-to-Earnings (TTM) ratio of -15.93, placing it in the 28.0% historical percentile (2026-09-29 News).

Varta

  • Insolvency Proceedings: Varta AG entered preliminary insolvency in July 2026. As of September 20, the company has announced the closure of its Nördlingen factory as part of a restructuring plan overseen by administrator Tobias Wahl (2026-09-20 News).
  • Consumer Unit Independence: Varta Consumer Batteries clarified that its retail battery business operates normally and is not legally affected by the insolvency proceedings of the parent, Varta AG (2026-09-21 News).

ZincFive

  • Corporate Merger: Filed a Form S-4 registration statement on September 30, 2026, for a proposed business combination with Spark I Acquisition Corp to facilitate a Delaware domestication and public listing (2026-09-30 News).
  • Product Launch: Released a purpose-built Battery Management System (BMS) and expanded into AI Dynamic Power Modules (DPM) and in-rack energy storage for data centers (2026-09-28 News).

3-Circles

  • Regulatory Enforcement: Shanghai market regulators and police dismantled three illegal "hand-assembled" lithium battery workshops using retired cells from new energy vehicles (2026-09-20 News).

AUDIO

Dali
