EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bang & Olufsen A/S (B&O) reported significant regulatory ownership fluctuations involving UBS Group AG, which briefly crossed the 5% disclosure threshold. The company is actively executing its DKK 33 million share buyback programme, repurchasing over 51,000 shares in early October to hedge incentive-based compensation. Following these transactions, the company maintains a treasury share holding of 2.6% of total voting rights.
