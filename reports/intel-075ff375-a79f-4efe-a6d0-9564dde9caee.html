Intelligence Briefing

HEADLINE

Energizer Holdings reported a significant decline in quarterly net earnings despite a marginal increase in top-line revenue for the fiscal third quarter of 2026.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Net sales for the quarter rose 1.2% to $734.1 million compared to the prior-year period. Profitability was pressured by a 39.3% increase in cost of products sold, resulting in a 29.8% decrease in gross profit and a 69.7% drop in pre-tax income. Basic EPS fell to $0.58, a 73.1% decline from $2.16 in the prior-year quarter.

KEY METRICS

MARGINS

  • Gross Margin: 38.2% (vs. 55.1% in prior-year quarter)
  • Operating Margin: 13.9% (vs. 30.3% in prior-year quarter)
  • Net Margin: 5.4% (vs. 21.2% in prior-year quarter)

BALANCE SHEET & CASH FLOW

  • Cash and Equivalents: $173.4 million, down 26.6% from the prior fiscal year-end.
  • Inventories: $747.8 million, a decrease of 4.3% since the prior fiscal year-end.
  • Long-term Debt: $3,294.9 million, reduced by 3.3% from $3,407.9 million at fiscal year-end.
  • Shareholders Equity: $203.2 million, up from $173.2 million in the preceding quarter.
  • Year-to-Date Operating Cash Flow: $156.0 million, an increase of 82.2% compared to the prior-year period.
  • Capital Expenditures (YTD): $52.1 million, down 24.6% year-over-year.
  • Share Repurchases (YTD): $5.4 million, representing a 91.4% reduction from the $62.6 million repurchased in the prior-year period.

OBSERVATIONS

  • Cost of products sold increased by $127.9 million (39.3%) year-over-year, significantly outpacing the 1.2% growth in net sales.
  • Year-to-date operating cash flow improved by $70.4 million despite the sharp decline in net earnings during the same period.
  • The company reduced research and development spending by 9.8% and advertising and promotion spending by 3.9% compared to the prior-year quarter.
  • Interest expense remained relatively stable with a modest 1.8% increase to $39.7 million.
  • Long-term debt represents approximately 73.8% of total assets as of June 30, 2026.
