Battery Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Panasonic Holdings significantly upgraded its fiscal 2027 profit outlook following a surge in earnings from its Energy unit, primarily driven by North American electric vehicle (EV) battery sales and data-center storage systems. Concurrently, the group is committing ¥92 billion to expand manufacturing and R&D for AI-related electronic components across China, Thailand, Indonesia, and Japan. Separately, Nanfu Battery's parent company, Anhui Anfu Battery Technology, reported a 76.93% increase in net profit for the first nine months of 2026, citing strong performance within the core battery business.

KEY INTELLIGENCE

  • Panasonic Energy Unit Performance: The unit reported a Q2 operating profit of 55.6 billion yen, a significant increase from 1.2 billion yen the previous year. The full-year profit forecast for the Energy unit was raised by 16% to 198 billion yen, driven by robust demand for EV batteries in North America and energy storage systems for data centers.
