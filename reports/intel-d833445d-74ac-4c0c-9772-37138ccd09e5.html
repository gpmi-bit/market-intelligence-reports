Battery Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Gold Peak Technology Group (parent of GP Batteries) has scheduled a board meeting for November 19 to approve interim financial results and consider dividend payments. Panasonic Holdings significantly raised its annual profit forecast following a surge in North American energy storage and EV battery earnings, contrasting with its Indian subsidiary which reported losses due to a factory fire and regulatory audit qualifications. Varta and FDK both signaled operational contraction, with the former closing a German production site and the latter recording impairments on alkaline manufacturing assets.

KEY INTELLIGENCE

  • Gold Peak Technology Group will convene its board on November 19, 2026, to approve unaudited interim results for the six months ended September 30. The meeting will also determine potential interim dividend payouts for the group, which operates production facilities in China, Vietnam, and Malaysia under its GP Batteries subsidiary. Read Source
  • Panasonic Holdings upwardly revised its group net profit forecast by 20% to ¥540 billion for the fiscal year ending March 2027. This adjustment follows an operating profit surge in its Energy segment (¥55.6 billion vs. ¥1.2 billion YoY), driven by North American EV battery demand and data-center energy storage sales. Read Source
  • Panasonic Energy India reported a ₹15.49 crore quarterly loss, citing a ₹7.26 crore exceptional loss from a fire at its Pithampur factory. Statutory auditors issued a qualified review because the company has not yet provided for obligations under India’s Battery Waste Management Rules. Read Source
  • VARTA Micro Production GmbH officially shuttered its Nördlingen, Germany, facility on October 31, 2026, following the loss of a major customer. The entity is one of four VARTA AG companies currently in insolvency proceedings; notably, the VARTA Consumer Batteries business remains excluded from these proceedings. Read Source
  • FDK recorded a net loss of ¥103 million for the half-year ended September 30, despite a rise in battery-segment sales to ¥24.442 billion. The loss was attributed to a ¥207 million impairment on alkaline battery manufacturing assets at its Washizu plant in Japan. Read Source
  • Energizer Holdings saw a reduction in institutional backing as FMR LLC (Fidelity) and Abigail P. Johnson disclosed a decreased beneficial ownership stake of 4.6% (approximately 3.16 million shares), down from 5.7% in June 2026. Read Source

ACTION ITEMS

  • Monitor the November 19 board meeting of Gold Peak Technology Group for specific performance data regarding GP Batteries' regional production output in Vietnam and Malaysia to gauge supply chain stability.
  • Brief the compliance and regional leadership teams on the qualified audit findings at Panasonic Energy India regarding the "Battery Waste Management Rules" to ensure GP Batteries is insulated from similar regulatory scrutiny in the Indian market.
  • Evaluate competitive market share opportunities in the alkaline category following FDK's asset impairment and Varta's production facility closure in Germany, which may indicate a shift in the primary battery manufacturing landscape.
