Audio Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Gold Peak Technology Group reported a 12.5% year-on-year increase in KEF sales for the first half of fiscal 2026/2027, driven by strong performance in Asian markets and successful new product launches. Bang & Olufsen continued its share buyback program to hedge incentive schemes and noted that UBS Group AG has reduced its holdings to 4.48%. Bose has issued a voluntary recall for approximately 670,000 battery packs used in its S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ portable speakers due to fire and overheating risks.

KEY INTELLIGENCE

  • KEF Financial Performance: Gold Peak Technology Group reported that its electronics and acoustics segment, anchored by KEF, saw revenue rise 6.9% to HK$868.1 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026. KEF sales specifically grew by 12.5%, contributing to a segment profit increase from HK$17.5 million to HK$68.6 million. Read Source
  • Regional Divergence and Tariff Impact: While Gold Peak recorded a 17.3% revenue increase in Asia, group-wide U.S. sales fell 13.3% due to tariff uncertainties. Previous filings indicate that approximately 45% of KEF sales in the U.S. market consist of imported products. Read Source
  • KEF Strategic Expansion: Management intends to strengthen KEF’s premium positioning by expanding experience centers in major cities and developing new partnerships with major retailers. The group is also seeking to diversify revenue through brand licensing and broadened global sales channels. Read Source
  • Bose Mass Battery Recall: Bose is recalling roughly 670,000 lithium-ion battery packs for S1 Pro portable speakers across the U.S., Canada, and Europe. The recall follows seven global reports of battery overheating and fire; users are advised to remove the batteries and utilize AC power until replacements are provided. Read Source
  • Bang & Olufsen Equity Movements: UBS Group AG reduced its combined holding of B&O shares and voting rights to 4.48% on November 12, 2026, falling below the 5% disclosure threshold. Simultaneously, B&O purchased 235,000 treasury shares between November 9 and 13 as part of its ongoing DKK 33 million buyback program. Read Source

ACTION ITEMS

  • Monitor Supply Chain Adjustments: Evaluate KEF's logistics and manufacturing footprint to mitigate the 13.3% decline in U.S. revenue attributed to ongoing tariff uncertainty.
  • Audit Product Safety Protocols: Review battery sourcing and thermal management testing for all portable audio lines to avoid the large-scale property damage and recall costs currently impacting Bose.
  • Assess Retail Footprint: Track the rollout of KEF experience centers in major Asian cities to determine the efficacy of the "premium-brand positioning" strategy on gross margins.
