Battery Brand Global Weekly Report

Executive Summary

Today’s battery market is defined by aggressive consolidation and strategic portfolio shifts as major players navigate brand transitions and regulatory hurdles. Panasonic’s divestiture of its ¥1 trillion power tool segment and Energizer’s rapid integration of Advanced Power Solutions (APS) in Europe signal a period of intense restructuring to focus on core energy storage and industrial scale. Meanwhile, Varta’s entry into the residential BESS market and next-generation sodium-ion consortia highlights a shift toward high-margin, integrated technology ecosystems.

Key Intelligence

  • Panasonic Divests Power Tool Division to Makita: In a major industrial consolidation, Makita Corporation will take full ownership of Panasonic Electric Works’ power tool operations. The deal, involving over 31,000 employees and a segment generating approximately $6.3 billion (¥1 trillion) in FY2025, allows Panasonic to refocus on electrical equipment and battery supply chains while Makita integrates Panasonic’s precision fastening with its own battery technology. Read Source
  • Energizer Reports $30M+ Organic Growth from APS Integration: Energizer Holdings confirmed the integration of Advanced Power Solutions (APS)—the former Panasonic battery manufacturing arm in Europe—is driving significant scale. Management expects more than $30 million in organic growth for FY2026 as European customers transition from Panasonic to Energizer branding. Read Source
  • Varta Launches Integrated Residential BESS and Joins Sodium-Ion Consortium: Moving beyond standalone cells, Varta launched the "Varta.hybrid.wall," an all-in-one residential energy storage system featuring an integrated inverter and modular capacity (4.5 kWh to 20 kWh). Separately, Varta Storage GmbH joined the SIB:DE project, Germany’s largest consortium for sodium-ion battery production, backed by €14.5 million in federal funding. Read Source 1 | Read Source 2
  • Anfu Technology (Nanfu) Net Profit Jumps 34%: The parent company of Nanfu Battery reported 2025 revenue of RMB 4.775 billion, with net profit rising 34.38% to RMB 226 million. Anfu has increased its equity interest in Nanfu to 46.02%, noting that while overseas revenue grew by 43.92%, it remains heavily dependent on low-margin OEM business (17.4% gross margin) compared to domestic operations (59.5%). Read Source
  • Rayovac Redesigns Packaging Following Regulatory Backlash: Under the Energizer umbrella, Rayovac will unveil new hearing aid battery packaging at the AAA conference in San Antonio. The redesign addresses public criticism regarding the difficulty of opening child-safe packaging mandated in 2024, aiming to restore accessibility for seniors while maintaining safety compliance. Read Source
  • Varta US Gigafactory Project Delayed to 2028: Forge Nano, an EV technology firm previously linked to Varta’s US expansion ambitions, has delayed its 200-job North Carolina facility to early 2028. The company cited a pending SPAC IPO and federal funding reviews as primary causes for the postponement. Read Source
  • Panasonic Energy Secures Lithium Supply for Kansas Facility: Panasonic Energy signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Frontier Lithium for future offtake. The agreement supports the supply chain for Panasonic’s $4 billion Kansas gigafactory, which entered mass production in 2025 to serve the North American EV market. Read Source
  • Spectrum Brands (Rayovac) to Release Q2 Results May 7: Spectrum Brands will report fiscal second-quarter 2026 results following a significant Q1 beat. Analysts are monitoring the brand’s ability to sustain momentum in the Americas despite ongoing tariff headwinds. Read Source
