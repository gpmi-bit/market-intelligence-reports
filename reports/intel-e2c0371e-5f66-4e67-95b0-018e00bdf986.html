Battery Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Energizer has released its fiscal Q2 2026 report, which noted an earnings beat supported by significant tariff refunds despite a slight decline in net sales. Anfutech, the parent company of Nanfu Battery, hit its 10% daily limit-up following the announcement of strong profit growth and the completion of major asset restructuring. These events signal a period of high financial volatility and operational pivoting among the industry's largest players.

KEY INTELLIGENCE

  • Energizer Holdings (NYSE: ENR) reported Q2 net sales of $643.3 million, down 3% year-over-year. Despite the revenue dip, adjusted EPS of $0.94 doubled consensus estimates, primarily driven by a $47.6 million tariff refund and a 360-basis-point expansion in adjusted gross margin to 44.4%. Read Source
  • Anfutech Co. Ltd. (SH: 603031), the parent of Nanfu Battery, hit the 10% daily limit-up on May 7, 2026, with market capitalization reaching ¥17.43 billion. The surge follows a reported 34.38% increase in 2025 net profit and the completion of restructuring that raised its equity stake in Nanfu to 46.02%. Read Source
  • FDK Corporation (TSE: 6955) reported that net profit for FY2025 rose 39.0% to ¥0.74 billion, despite a 5.7% decline in total net sales to ¥59.56 billion. The company simultaneously announced its "R3" mid-term plan, which targets ¥80 billion in net sales by FY2029 through a pivot toward new business segments. Read Source
  • Duracell must face a federal trade secrets lawsuit filed by German chemical company BASF after a U.S. District Judge rejected Duracell's motion to dismiss. The litigation centers on allegations that Duracell misappropriated proprietary lithium-ion battery cathode technology during a collaboration agreement. Read Source
  • Duracell has listed its 311,000-square-foot North American headquarters in Bethel, Connecticut, for $17 million. The facility divestiture marks a strategic geographic shift as the company prepares to relocate its research and development operations to Atlanta, Georgia, by summer 2026. Read Source
  • Energizer launched its "Ultimate Child Shield" lithium coin batteries, featuring a bitter-taste coating and a color-alert dye that stains a child’s mouth blue upon contact with saliva. Management expects this safety innovation to be a key driver for organic net sales growth in the second half of 2026. Read Source

ACTION ITEMS

  • Monitor the competitive response to Energizer's safety-led innovation in the lithium coin segment; assess if this technology becomes a mandatory regulatory standard in key markets.
  • Evaluate the risk to Duracell's lithium-ion roadmap following the federal court’s refusal to dismiss the BASF trade secrets lawsuit, which now enters the discovery phase.
  • Analyze Anfutech's increased control over Nanfu Battery for potential shifts in export pricing or aggressive expansion of the Nanfu brand into global alkaline markets.
