EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

GP Energy Tech, a subsidiary of Gold Peak Technology Group, debuted its nickel-zinc (NiZn) battery systems at the Hong Kong Autumn Electronics Fair, targeting AI and cloud data center infrastructure. The company confirmed that its Dongguan, China manufacturing facility reached full mass production in June 2026 and completed its first commercial delivery in August. To enhance supply resilience, the organization is executing a "Manufacturing Twins" strategy, with a second production facility scheduled to open in Malaysia by Q3 2027.
