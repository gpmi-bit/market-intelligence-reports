Battery Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

GP Industries Limited reported a 10.3% revenue increase in its battery business for the first half of fiscal 2026/2027, driven by high demand for cylindrical alkaline and coin-cell products despite a 15.6% decline in U.S. sales. Panasonic Holdings is repositioning its Chinese energy operations into technology hubs and converting Japanese automotive production lines to serve the expanding data-center backup market. Duracell and Nanfu Battery reported revenue growth and new credit guarantees, respectively, while navigating increased administrative costs and high liability-to-asset ratios in marketing subsidiaries.

KEY INTELLIGENCE

  • GP Industries reported group revenue of S$606.5 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026, with battery revenue rising 10.3% to S$464.6 million. Growth was led by a 16.0% increase in cylindrical alkaline sales and a 22.6% surge in coin-cell demand. Read Source
  • GP Industries faced a battery-segment margin contraction from 24.5% to 23.4% due to reduced VAT export rebates in China, transitional staff costs, and higher logistics expenses. The company announced plans to rightsize operations and expand production capacity outside China to mitigate trade risks and tariff-policy uncertainty. Read Source
  • Panasonic Holdings reported a 45% year-over-year increase in Energy segment sales to ¥645.9 billion. The company is pivoting its Wuxi and Suzhou sites in China from pure manufacturing bases to R&D and technology hubs, with over 90% of their output currently destined for export. Read Source
  • Panasonic management confirmed the conversion of Japanese automotive battery lines to produce data-center backup units to meet tight global supply. The company noted that battery cells comprise less than 20% of the cost of these units, with high-margin value residing in the modules and system solutions. Read Source
  • Berkshire Hathaway disclosed that Duracell revenue increased during Q3 2026, though pretax earnings for the consumer products group fell 29.2%. This decline was attributed to the non-recurrence of large 2025 production tax credits and costs related to relocating administrative facilities. Read Source
  • Anfu Technology approved RMB 89.4 million in joint-and-several guarantees provided by Fujian Nanping Nanfu Battery for its marketing subsidiary. The subsidiary’s liabilities-to-assets ratio currently exceeds 70%, prompting management to implement tighter monitoring of repayment risks. Read Source

ACTION ITEMS

  • Accelerate Supply Chain Diversification: Execute the planned expansion of production facilities outside of China to reduce exposure to reduced VAT export rebates and fluctuating U.S. tariff policies.
  • Optimize Product Mix for Margin Recovery: Prioritize the high-growth cylindrical alkaline and coin-cell segments to offset rising logistics costs and transitional restructuring expenses.
  • Monitor Subsidiary Debt Levels: Implement stringent oversight of marketing and distribution subsidiaries with high debt-to-asset ratios to ensure that group-wide credit guarantees do not exceed authorized limits.
