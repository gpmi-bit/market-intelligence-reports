Battery Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The global battery sector is undergoing a strategic pivot toward AI infrastructure and data center energy storage to offset stagnating electric vehicle (EV) demand and retail volatility. Gold Peak (GP) and Panasonic are aggressively scaling Nickel Zinc (NiZn) and specialized lithium production for the UPS and data center markets, while Energizer is relying on one-time tariff refunds to mask organic revenue declines. Competitive focus is shifting from mass-market EV supply to high-margin energy services, evidenced by Duracell's expansion into UK charging infrastructure and Nanfu’s leadership transition aimed at finding new growth curves.
