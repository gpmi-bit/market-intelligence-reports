Audio Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bose has initiated a global recall of over 670,000 lithium-ion battery packs for its S1 Pro portable speaker systems following reports of overheating and fire hazards. Bang & Olufsen reported a temporary fluctuation in major shareholding by UBS Group AG while continuing the execution of its planned share buyback program. Klipsch and Onkyo, operating under Gentex’s Premium Audio division, saw a 27% year-over-year revenue increase in Q3 2026, supported by a recovery in the Australian distribution channel.

KEY INTELLIGENCE

  • Bose Safety Recall: Bose has issued a joint recall with Health Canada and the U.S. CPSC for approximately 634,759 battery packs in the U.S. and 35,399 in Canada used in S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ systems. The company received seven reports of the units melting or catching fire; affected serial prefixes include 083723, 078352, 084105, 078591, 078592, 084106, and 086409. Read Source
  • Expansion of Bose Recall to Australia and Europe: The recall extends to the Australian and Italian markets, where customers are advised to stop using the battery packs immediately and operate speakers via AC power until a free replacement is provided. While specific unit counts for these regions were not disclosed, the same 17-digit serial number prefixes are being used to identify faulty hardware globally. Read Source
  • Klipsch and Onkyo Financial Recovery: Gentex’s Premium Audio business reported Q3 revenue of US$54.7 million, a 27% increase from the previous year’s US$43.0 million. Growth was largely attributed to the Reference Premiere speaker lines and the Flexus and Powered Speaker series (Fives, Sevens, Nines). Read Source
  • B&O Shareholding Volatility: UBS Group AG briefly crossed the 5% ownership threshold in Bang & Olufsen on October 30, 2026, before reducing its position to 4.66% (6,866,831 shares) by November 2. Read Source
  • B&O Capital Management: Bang & Olufsen repurchased 30,000 shares between October 26 and October 30 at an average price of DKK 10.57. The company now holds 4,629,918 treasury shares, representing 3.1% of its total share capital, as part of a program to hedge share-based incentive obligations. Read Source

ACTION ITEMS

  • Monitor Competitive Displacement: Brief sales teams on the Bose S1 Pro recall to identify opportunities where customers may seek alternative professional portable PA systems with verified battery safety standards.
  • Track Distribution Shifts: Evaluate the impact of the Klipsch and Onkyo recovery in the Australian market, specifically regarding their partnership with distributor Westan, to assess changes in regional market share for premium home audio.
  • Review Financial Disclosures: Audit Bang & Olufsen’s upcoming regulatory filings for further institutional ownership changes to gauge investor sentiment regarding their current capital management and buyback strategy.
