Battery Brand Global Weekly Report

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Duracell has officially opened its US$56 million Global Innovation Center in Atlanta to centralize research into materials science and cell design. VARTA Consumer Batteries has successfully relaunched as an independent entity following a government-approved spin-off from the insolvent VARTA AG, while Panasonic Energy is pivoting its lithium-ion and supercapacitor technology toward AI data center infrastructure and humanoid robotics. GP Energy Tech and Energizer are prioritizing high-growth segments, with the former expanding nickel-based battery production for data centers and the latter securing a significant legal victory in US antitrust litigation.

KEY INTELLIGENCE

  • Duracell Establishes Global R&D Hub: The company inaugurated an 83,000-square-foot Global Innovation Center at Science Square Labs in Atlanta. The US$56 million facility will serve as the anchor for global research and development, focusing on battery performance, safety, and laboratory prototyping. Read Source
  • VARTA Consumer Batteries Gains Independence: Following approval from Germany’s Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs, VARTA Consumer Batteries GmbH has been spun off from the insolvent VARTA AG Group. The now-independent company plans 3-4% annual sales growth through 2029 and will maintain its primary manufacturing base in Dischingen. Read Source
  • Panasonic Accelerates Next-Generation Technology Timelines: The company confirmed it will begin shipping small-format all-solid-state battery samples in Q4 2026 for industrial machinery. Additionally, Panasonic Holdings aims to produce humanoid robots by 2029, utilizing its cylindrical lithium-ion technology to meet high safety and endurance requirements.   • GP Energy Tech Targets AI Data Center Market: Gold Peak’s subsidiary is investing US$150 million in the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone to manufacture nickel-based batteries. The move aligns with China's "15th Five-Year Plan" for batteries, targeting high-drain uninterruptible power supply (UPS) applications for AI data centers. Read Source
  • Energizer Prevails in Antitrust Class Action: A US District Court denied class certification in a consolidated antitrust case alleging price-fixing between Energizer and Walmart. Econometric evidence successfully rebutted claims of class-wide harm, removing the immediate threat of significant damages. Read Source
  • Panasonic Energy Pivots to Data Center Infrastructure: The group is expanding beyond automotive cells to provide complete energy storage systems for data centers. The strategy involves combining lithium-ion batteries with supercapacitors to manage the high-power fluctuations required by AI computing clusters. Read Source
  • Nanfu Parent Issues Financial Guarantees: Anhui Anfu Battery Technology (Nanfu’s parent) disclosed three new joint-liability guarantee contracts totaling RMB 200 million. As of late September 2026, the company’s total guarantee balance stands at RMB 2.07 billion, representing roughly 75% of its audited net assets. Read Source
  • Energizer Discloses Lobbying and Safety Benchmarks: The company spent US$80,000 on Q3 2026 federal lobbying, focusing on trade tariffs and battery safety regulations. Its "Ultimate Child Shield" coin cell was recently highlighted by Consumer Reports as a safety benchmark for preventing internal chemical burns. Read Source 1 | Read Source 2
